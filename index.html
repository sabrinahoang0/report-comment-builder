    How to lay out a comment bank

    Word: give each part a heading (for example “1. Opening”, “2. Participation”, “3. Progress”, “Optional add-ons”, “4. Reflection”), with a table under it. The last column of each table is the sentence; the columns before it are its label, such as the grade (“Outstanding (A)”, “B”, “C–D”) and, for Progress, the area (“Speaking”, “Writing”). Any other heading with a table, such as “Hiragana”, becomes an extra part of the comment, in the order it appears. Headings like “How to use” and “Sample comments” are skipped.

    Spreadsheet: one row per sentence, with the columns Part, Label, Area (optional, for Progress) and Comment. Download the template to see an example.

    In the sentences, use {first_name}, {he_she}, {his_her}, {him_her} and {himself_herself}; they are filled in for each student. Put grades A to E in the labels so the right sentences are offered. Reflection labels starting “Weak” (for example “Weak writing”) are used when that area is a student's lowest mark, and a Reflection labelled “Focus” suits distracted students. Words in [square brackets] are left for you to fill in.
